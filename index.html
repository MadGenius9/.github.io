Mobile Application Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 19, 2026

HitchTrack ("we," "our," or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use the HitchTrack mobile application (the "Application") designed to track shift rotations and work calendars.

Please read this privacy policy carefully. If you do not agree with the terms of this privacy policy, please do not access the application.

1. Information We Collect

We only collect information that is strictly necessary to provide and improve the functionality of the Application. This includes:

  • User-Inputted Data: Calendar schedules, shift patterns, hitch rotations, and work notes that you input directly into the Application. This data is stored locally on your device.
  • Third-Party Financial Integrations (Plaid API): If you choose to connect your financial accounts to use premium features like budgeting or income forecasting, we utilize the Plaid API. Your financial institution credentials are entered directly into Plaid’s secure interface. HitchTrack never sees, receives, or stores your raw banking passwords. Plaid provides us with a secure, read-only token to access transaction data needed for the application's budgeting features.
  • Device Information: Basic, non-identifying technical data such as your device model, operating system version, and unique device identifiers necessary for crash reporting and app performance monitoring.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information collected through the Application to:

  • Generate and maintain your custom work rotation schedules and calendars.
  • Process financial data streams safely through Plaid to populate your application dashboards.
  • Monitor and analyze application usage, identify software bugs, and optimize system performance.
  • Enforce our terms, conditions, and anti-fraud protocols.

3. Disclosure of Your Information

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. We may share information we have collected about you in certain situations, strictly limited to:

  • By Law or to Protect Rights: If we believe the release of information about you is necessary to respond to legal processes, protect user safety, or address fraudulent activity.
  • Third-Party Service Providers: We share data with trusted third-party processors, specifically Plaid Inc., to deliver financial integration features. All third-party providers are contractually obligated to maintain strict data confidentiality.

4. Data Storage and Security

Your calendar entries, shift logs, and custom configurations are stored locally on your physical device. We implement industry-standard administrative, technical, and physical security measures to protect any data transmitted to our systems. However, please be aware that despite our structural efforts, no security measures are completely impenetrable, and no method of data transmission can be guaranteed against interception.

5. Children's Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal data from children under the age of 13, nor is the application marketed to children. If we learn that any personal information has been collected from a child under age 13 without verifiable parental consent, we will delete that data from our infrastructure immediately.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We reserve the right to make changes to this Privacy Policy at any time and for any reason. We will alert you about any changes by updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy page. You are encouraged to periodically review this Privacy Policy to stay informed of updates.
